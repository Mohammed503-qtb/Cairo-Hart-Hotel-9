     1→# HOTEL PLATFORM — MASTER PLAN
     2→## Integrated Hotel Booking, PMS, Guest Journey, Guest App & Reception Operations
     3→
     4→> **Document Type:** Master Product & Engineering Plan  
     5→> **Status:** Approved Scope / Execution Baseline  
     6→> **Version:** 1.0  
     7→> **Primary Goal:** Build a coherent hotel operating platform that connects the public hotel website, booking engine, reservation lifecycle, reception/PMS operations, guest application, guest service requests, room lifecycle, billing, notifications, WhatsApp communication, and auditability into one integrated system.
     8→
     9→---
    10→
    11→# 0. EXECUTIVE DEFINITION
    12→
    13→This project is **not** merely a hotel website.
    14→
    15→This project is **not** merely an online booking system.
    16→
    17→This project is **not** merely a guest mobile application.
    18→
    19→This project is an **integrated hotel operating platform** in which the guest experiences one continuous journey:
    20→
    21→```text
    22→Discover Hotel
    23→    ↓
    24→Browse Rooms
    25→    ↓
    26→Check Availability
    27→    ↓
    28→Create Reservation
    29→    ↓
    30→Payment / Payment Method
    31→    ↓
    32→Reservation Confirmation
    33→    ↓
    34→WhatsApp Confirmation + Booking File
    35→    ↓
    36→Pre-Arrival Communication
    37→    ↓
    38→Guest Arrives
    39→    ↓
    40→Reception Verifies Guest
    41→    ↓
    42→Check-In
    43→    ↓
    44→Reservation becomes Active Stay
    45→    ↓
    46→Room becomes Occupied
    47→    ↓
    48→Guest Access to Hotel App
    49→    ↓
    50→Guest Uses Room + Hotel Services
    51→    ↓
    52→Requests / Messages / Problems / Housekeeping / Services
    53→    ↓
    54→Reception Receives and Manages Requests
    55→    ↓
    56→Operations Execute Requests
    57→    ↓
    58→Charges / Payments / Adjustments
    59→    ↓
    60→Extension / Room Change / Other Stay Changes
    61→    ↓
    62→Pre-Checkout
    63→    ↓
    64→Checkout
    65→    ↓
    66→Room becomes Dirty
    67→    ↓
    68→Housekeeping / Inspection
    69→    ↓
    70→Room becomes Available
    71→    ↓
    72→Stay Closed
    73→    ↓
    74→Guest Feedback / Post-Stay Communication
    75→```
    76→
    77→The platform must maintain a **single source of truth** for the guest, reservation, stay, room, requests, charges, payments, and operational history.

    81→# 1. PRODUCT VISION
    82→
    83→## 1.1 Vision
    84→
    85→Create a hotel platform where the guest can move from discovery to completed stay with minimal friction, while hotel staff can operate reservations, rooms, guest requests, check-in, check-out, billing, and communication from one central operational system.
    86→
    87→## 1.2 Core Principle
    88→
    89→A guest action must not become an isolated message.
    90→
    91→For example:
    92→
    93→```text
    94→Guest clicks "Request Cleaning"
    95→```
    96→
    97→must become:
    98→
    99→```text
   100→Guest
   101→    ↓
   102→Current Stay
   103→    ↓
   104→Room
   105→    ↓
   106→Guest Request
   107→    ↓
   108→Operational Assignment
   109→    ↓
   110→Status Tracking
   111→    ↓
   112→Completion
   113→    ↓
   114→Guest Notification
   115→    ↓
   116→Optional Charge
   117→    ↓
   118→Audit Log
   119→```
   120→
   121→The request must remain traceable from creation until closure.

   417→# 6. RESERVATION VS STAY
   418→
   419→This distinction is mandatory.
   420→
   421→## Reservation
   422→
   423→Represents a booking before and around arrival.
   424→
   425→Example:
   426→
   427→```text
   428→Reservation #HTL-2026-000421
   429→Guest: Mohamed Ahmed
   430→Check-in: 2026-09-01
   431→Check-out: 2026-09-04
   432→Room Type: Deluxe Double
   433→Status: Confirmed
   434→```
   435→
   436→## Stay
   437→
   438→Represents the guest's actual hotel occupancy after successful check-in.
   439→
   440→Example:
   441→
   442→```text
   443→Stay #ST-2026-000883
   444→Reservation: HTL-2026-000421
   445→Guest: Mohamed Ahmed
   446→Room: 204
   447→Status: In-House
   448→```
   449→
   450→### MUST NOT
   451→
   452→Do not use Reservation as a substitute for Stay after check-in.
   453→
   454→A guest request during an active stay must be linked to the active Stay.

   525→

   539→## 8.3 Price Calculation
   540→
   541→Pricing pipeline:
   542→
   543→```text
   544→Base Rate
   545→+ Extra Occupancy Charges (if applicable)
   546→+ Applicable Service/Tax
   547→- Discounts
   548→= Total
   549→```
   550→
   551→Every calculated amount must be reproducible.
   552→

   557→# 9. RESERVATION LIFECYCLE
   558→
   559→Suggested states:
   560→
   561→```text
   562→Draft
   563→Pending
   564→Confirmed
   565→Cancelled
   566→No-Show
   567→Checked-In
   568→Checked-Out
   569→Completed
   570→```
   571→
   572→Rules:
   573→
   574→- `Draft` is not inventory-confirmed until appropriate.
   575→- `Confirmed` reserves applicable inventory.
   576→- `Cancelled` releases reserved inventory according to business rules.
   577→- `No-Show` must follow hotel policy.
   578→- `Checked-In` means the guest has entered operational stay.
   579→- `Checked-Out` means stay is closed operationally.
   580→- `Completed` is an optional finalized historical state.
   581→
   582→Every state transition must be logged.

   713→# 13. GUEST ACCESS / UNIQUE CODE
   714→
   715→The unique access code is not a normal hotel-wide password.
   716→
   717→It should be an **activation credential tied to a specific guest stay**.
   718→
   719→## 13.1 Requirements
   720→
   721→- Unique enough to prevent guessing.
   722→- Time/validity controlled.
   723→- Can be revoked.
   724→- Can be regenerated.
   725→- Must not expose internal IDs.
   726→- Must be rate-limited.
   727→- Must not be reusable indefinitely.
   728→- Must be tied to the correct stay or approved activation context.
   729→
   730→## 13.2 Activation
   731→
   732→Guest opens app:
   733→
   734→```text
   735→Enter Hotel Access Code
   736→```
   737→
   738→System validates:
   739→
   740→```text
   741→Code
   742→↓
   743→Validity
   744→↓
   745→Hotel
   746→↓
   747→Stay
   748→↓
   749→Guest
   750→↓
   751→Current Room
   752→```
   753→
   754→Then creates a normal authenticated session/token for the guest.
   755→
   756→The raw access code must not be treated as the permanent authentication credential.

   848→# 17. REQUEST WORKFLOW
   849→
   850→Every request must be stateful.
   851→
   852→Recommended states:
   853→
   854→```text
   855→NEW
   856→ACKNOWLEDGED
   857→ASSIGNED
   858→IN_PROGRESS
   859→WAITING
   860→COMPLETED
   861→CANCELLED
   862→REJECTED
   863→REOPENED
   864→```
   865→
   866→## 17.1 Lifecycle
   867→
   868→```text
   869→Guest Creates Request
   870→        ↓
   871→NEW
   872→        ↓
   873→Reception Receives
   874→        ↓
   875→ACKNOWLEDGED
   876→        ↓
   877→Assigned / In Progress
   878→        ↓
   879→COMPLETED
   880→        ↓
   881→Guest Notified
   882→        ↓
   883→Guest Confirms / Reopens if necessary
   884→```
   885→
   886→## 17.2 Request Object Must Store
   887→
   888→- Request ID.
   889→- Stay ID.
   890→- Guest ID.
   891→- Room ID.
   892→- Category.
   893→- Service.
   894→- Description.
   895→- Priority.
   896→- Created time.
   897→- Assigned user/team.
   898→- Status.
   899→- Completion time.
   900→- Notes.
