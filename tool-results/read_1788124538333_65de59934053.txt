    10→    10→
    11→    11→# 0. EXECUTIVE DEFINITION
    12→    12→
    13→    13→This project is **not** merely a hotel website.
    14→    14→
    15→    15→This project is **not** merely an online booking system.
    16→    16→
    17→    17→This project is **not** merely a guest mobile application.
    18→    18→
    19→    19→This project is an **integrated hotel operating platform** in which the guest experiences one continuous journey:
    20→    20→
    21→    21→```text
    22→    22→Discover Hotel
    23→    23→    ↓
    24→    24→Browse Rooms
    25→    25→    ↓
    26→    26→Check Availability
    27→    27→    ↓
    28→    28→Create Reservation
    29→    29→    ↓
    30→    30→Payment / Payment Method
    31→    31→    ↓
    32→    32→Reservation Confirmation
    33→    33→    ↓
    34→    34→WhatsApp Confirmation + Booking File
    35→    35→    ↓
    36→    36→Pre-Arrival Communication
    37→    37→    ↓
    38→    38→Guest Arrives
    39→    39→    ↓
    40→    40→Reception Verifies Guest
    41→    41→    ↓
    42→    42→Check-In
    43→    43→    ↓
    44→    44→Reservation becomes Active Stay
    45→    45→    ↓
    46→    46→Room becomes Occupied
    47→    47→    ↓
    48→    48→Guest Access to Hotel App
    49→    49→    ↓
    50→    50→Guest Uses Room + Hotel Services
    51→    51→    ↓
    52→    52→Requests / Messages / Problems / Housekeeping / Services
    53→    53→    ↓
    54→    54→Reception Receives and Manages Requests
    55→    55→    ↓
    56→    56→Operations Execute Requests
    57→    57→    ↓
    58→    58→Charges / Payments / Adjustments
    59→    59→    ↓
    60→    60→Extension / Room Change / Other Stay Changes
    61→    61→    ↓
    62→    62→Pre-Checkout
    63→    63→    ↓
    64→    64→Checkout
    65→    65→    ↓
    66→    66→Room becomes Dirty
    67→    67→    ↓
    68→    68→Housekeeping / Inspection
    69→    69→    ↓
    70→    70→Room becomes Available
    71→    71→    ↓
    72→    72→Stay Closed
    73→    73→    ↓
    74→    74→Guest Feedback / Post-Stay Communication
    75→    75→```
    76→    76→
    77→    77→The platform must maintain a **single source of truth** for the guest, reservation, stay, room, requests, charges, payments, and operational history.

    82→    82→
    83→    83→## 1.1 Vision
    84→    84→
    85→    85→Create a hotel platform where the guest can move from discovery to completed stay with minimal friction, while hotel staff can operate reservations, rooms, guest requests, check-in, check-out, billing, and communication from one central operational system.

    89→    89→A guest action must not become an isolated message.
    90→    90→
    91→    91→For example:
    92→    92→
    93→    93→```text
    94→    94→Guest clicks "Request Cleaning"
    95→    95→```
    96→    96→
    97→    97→must become:
    98→    98→
    99→    99→```text
   100→   100→Guest
   101→   101→    ↓
   102→   102→Current Stay
   103→   103→    ↓
   104→   104→Room
   105→   105→    ↓
   106→   106→Guest Request
   107→   107→    ↓
   108→   108→Operational Assignment
   109→   109→    ↓
   110→   110→Status Tracking
   111→   111→    ↓
   112→   112→Completion
   113→   113→    ↓
   114→   114→Guest Notification
   115→   115→    ↓
   116→   116→Optional Charge
   117→   117→    ↓
   118→   118→Audit Log
   119→   119→```
   120→   120→
   121→   121→The request must remain traceable from creation until closure.

   417→   417→# 6. RESERVATION VS STAY
   418→   418→
   419→   419→This distinction is mandatory.
   420→   420→
   421→   421→## Reservation
   422→   422→
   423→   423→Represents a booking before and around arrival.
   424→   424→
   425→   425→Example:
   426→   426→
   427→   427→```text
   428→   428→Reservation #HTL-2026-000421
   429→   429→Guest: Mohamed Ahmed
   430→   430→Check-in: 2026-09-01
   431→   431→Check-out: 2026-09-04
   432→   432→Room Type: Deluxe Double
   433→   433→Status: Confirmed
   434→   434→```
   435→   435→
   436→   436→## Stay
   437→   437→
   438→   438→Represents the guest's actual hotel occupancy after successful check-in.
   439→   439→
   440→   440→Example:
   441→   441→
   442→   442→```text
   443→   443→Stay #ST-2026-000883
   444→   444→Reservation: HTL-2026-000421
   445→   445→Guest: Mohamed Ahmed
   446→   446→Room: 204
   447→   447→Status: In-House
   448→   448→```
   449→   449→
   450→   450→### MUST NOT
   451→   451→
   452→   452→Do not use Reservation as a substitute for Stay after check-in.
   453→   453→
   454→   454→A guest request during an active stay must be linked to the active Stay.

   505→   505→# 8. BOOKING ENGINE
   506→   506→
   507→   507→## 8.1 Search
   508→   508→
   509→   509→Inputs:
   510→   510→

   512→   512→- Check-out date.
   513→   513→- Adults.
   514→   514→- Children if supported.
   515→   515→- Room quantity where supported.
   516→   516→
   517→   517→Validation:
   518→   518→
   519→   519→- Check-out > check-in.
   520→   520→- Dates allowed by hotel policy.
   521→   521→- Occupancy rules.
   522→   522→- Room availability.
   523→   523→
   524→   524→## 8.2 Availability Calculation
   525→   525→
   526→   526→Availability must consider:
   527→   527→
   528→   528→- Existing reservations.
   529→   529→- Active stays.
   530→   530→- Blocked rooms.
   531→   531→- Out-of-order rooms.
   532→   532→- Out-of-service rooms.
   533→   533→- Room inventory.
   534→   534→- Room type inventory.
   535→   535→- Date overlap.
   536→   536→
   537→   537→The system MUST NOT sell the same inventory twice.
   538→   538→
   539→   539→## 8.3 Price Calculation
   540→   540→
   541→   541→Pricing pipeline:
   542→   542→
   543→   543→```text
   544→   544→Base Rate
   545→   545→+ Extra Occupancy Charges (if applicable)
   546→   546→+ Applicable Service/Tax
   547→   547→- Discounts
   548→   548→= Total
   549→   549→```
   550→   550→
   551→   551→Every calculated amount must be reproducible.
   552→   552→
   553→   553→The system should store the booking-time pricing snapshot so later rate changes do not silently alter an existing confirmed reservation.
   554→   554→

   556→   556→
   557→   557→# 9. RESERVATION LIFECYCLE
   558→   558→
   559→   559→Suggested states:
   560→   560→
   561→   561→```text
   562→   562→Draft
   563→   563→Pending
   564→   564→Confirmed
   565→   565→Cancelled
   566→   566→No-Show
   567→   567→Checked-In
   568→   568→Checked-Out
   569→   569→Completed
   570→   570→```
   571→   571→
   572→   572→Rules:
   573→   573→
   574→   574→- `Draft` is not inventory-confirmed until appropriate.
   575→   575→- `Confirmed` reserves applicable inventory.
   576→   576→- `Cancelled` releases reserved inventory according to business rules.
   577→   577→- `No-Show` must follow hotel policy.
   578→   578→- `Checked-In` means the guest has entered operational stay.
   579→   579→- `Checked-Out` means stay is closed operationally.
   580→   580→- `Completed` is an optional finalized historical state.
   581→   581→
   582→   582→Every state transition must be logged.

   670→   670→- Reservation number.
   671→   671→- Guest.
   672→   672→- Room type.

   678→   678→
   679→   679→Reception:
   680→   680→
   681→   681→1. Searches reservation.
   682→   682→2. Verifies guest identity according to hotel policy.
   683→   683→3. Confirms reservation.

   690→   690→10. Creates guest access.
   691→   691→11. Sends/prints guest access information.
   692→   692→
   693→   693→## 12.3 Check-in Result
   694→   694→
   695→   695→Before:
   696→   696→
   697→   697→```text

   699→   699→Room = Reserved / Available according to setup

   713→   713→# 13. GUEST ACCESS / UNIQUE CODE
   714→   714→
   715→   715→The unique access code is not a normal hotel-wide password.
   716→   716→
   717→   717→It should be an **activation credential tied to a specific guest stay**.
   718→   718→
   719→   719→## 13.1 Requirements
   720→   720→
   721→   721→- Unique enough to prevent guessing.
   722→   722→- Time/validity controlled.
   723→   723→- Can be revoked.
   724→   724→- Can be regenerated.
   725→   725→- Must not expose internal IDs.
   726→   726→- Must be rate-limited.
   727→   727→- Must not be reusable indefinitely.
   728→   728→- Must be tied to the correct stay or approved activation context.
   729→   729→
   730→   730→## 13.2 Activation
   731→   731→
   732→   732→Guest opens app:
   733→   733→
   734→   734→```text
   735→   735→Enter Hotel Access Code
   736→   736→```
   737→   737→
   738→   738→System validates:
   739→   739→
   740→   740→```text
   741→   741→Code
   742→   742→↓
   743→   743→Validity
   744→   744→↓
   745→   745→Hotel
   746→   746→↓
   747→   747→Stay
   748→   748→↓
   749→   749→Guest
   750→   750→↓
   751→   751→Current Room
   752→   752→```
   753→   753→
   754→   754→Then creates a normal authenticated session/token for the guest.
   755→   755→
   756→   756→The raw access code must not be treated as the permanent authentication credential.
   757→   757→

  1760→  1760→# 45. CORE BUSINESS INVARIANTS
  1761→  1761→
  1762→  1762→These are non-negotiable.
  1763→  1763→
  1764→  1764→## Reservation / Inventory
  1765→  1765→
  1766→  1766→- A room cannot be double-booked.
  1767→  1767→- An unavailable room cannot be assigned without an authorized override.
  1768→  1768→- Cancelled reservations must not continue blocking inventory.
  1769→  1769→
  1770→  1770→## Stay
  1771→  1771→
  1772→  1772→- A stay requires a valid guest.
  1773→  1773→- A stay requires valid reservation context where applicable.
  1774→  1774→- An active stay must have a valid room assignment unless the workflow explicitly allows an unassigned state.
  1775→  1775→- A checked-out stay cannot continue receiving normal in-house requests.
  1776→  1776→
  1777→  1777→## Guest App
  1778→  1778→
  1779→  1779→- Guest only sees authorized current/historical information.
  1780→  1780→- Guest requests must belong to an authorized stay.
  1781→  1781→- Access must not persist incorrectly after checkout.
  1782→  1782→
  1783→  1783→## Billing
  1784→  1784→
  1785→  1785→- Every charge must have a source.
  1786→  1786→- Every payment must be auditable.
  1787→  1787→- Balance must be derivable from transactions.
  1788→  1788→- Client cannot directly set final balance.
  1789→  1789→
  1790→  1790→## Room
  1791→  1791→
  1792→  1792→- Room state transitions must be valid.
  1793→  1793→- Room cannot simultaneously be active in conflicting stays.
